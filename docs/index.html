How to use

  • Scrub to a frame where the segmentation is wrong. Pause. Click on the cell to place a correction point.
  • Neutrophil — click inside the cell body that IS the neutrophil.
  • RBC — click inside a cell that should be an RBC but isn't (or is being labelled wrong).
  • Neither — click on a region that is being wrongly labelled as a cell (background / debris).
  • Export corrections.json → place it in the output/ folder → re-run: python3 pipeline.py --corrections output/corrections.json
  • Markers are saved in browser localStorage automatically. Import/Export to persist across sessions.
  • Annotating ~10–20 frames spread across the video gives good coverage. Focus on frames where the neutrophil identity is ambiguous.
