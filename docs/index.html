Pipeline notes

  • RBCs detected via Hough Circle Transform (diameter ≈ 37px). Some edge/overlapping cells may be missed.
  • Neutrophil = largest foreground blob from rolling median background subtraction (25-frame window).
  • Microbe detected as very small dark blobs outside known cell regions. May include false positives on debris.
  • Frames with large inter-frame differences (stage moves) are marked "STAGE MOVE" and skipped.
  • Pure OpenCV pipeline — CPU only, ~18fps throughput, no GPU required.
